# Attention Matters visual system

## Brand idea

Attention Matters turns noisy, ambiguous business problems into a focused first move. The visual system pairs an editorial sense of calm with a precise, technical signal.

The personality is:

- practical, not theatrical
- senior, not corporate
- warm, not cute
- technical, not jargon-heavy
- focused, not sparse for its own sake

## Identity

The identity has two parts:

1. A plain-language `Attention Matters` wordmark.
2. A compact AM signal mark for favicons and social avatars.

The identity uses Irene's new angular AM logo: a blue-black A and teal M meet around a violet lightbulb. The open silhouette retains the original brand idea without placing it inside an app-icon tile. `public/am-logo.jpeg` is the supplied master; `public/am-logo-mark.webp` and `public/am-logo-lockup.webp` are its web-optimized crops. `public/social-avatar.png` is the upload-ready square avatar.

## Colour

The palette follows the 60–30–10 principle:

- 60% warm paper: `#FEF1E9`
- 30% blue-black structure: `#171624`
- under 10% digital signal accents:
  - violet: `#7157C8`
  - cyan: `#008DA1`
  - pale mint: `#A9E1DC`

Off-white carries long-form reading. Blue-black creates authority and anchors the main offer. Violet and cyan are separated into precise signals rather than blended into decorative gradients.

## Typography

Use self-hosted Instrument Sans Variable for a precise but human voice. Large headings are tightly tracked and confident; body copy keeps a generous, readable line height.

## Photography

Use Irene's portrait once at a restrained scale. Align it to the right of the About heading on desktop and beside the opening biography paragraph on mobile. The image should provide human trust without turning the hero into a personal-brand profile. Use a tight 4:5 head-and-shoulders crop with no background people, and avoid decorative masks or effects.

## Layout

- Maximum content width: 1180px
- Primary rhythm: large editorial sections with strong boundaries
- Desktop layouts use asymmetrical two-column compositions
- Mobile layouts collapse to one deliberate reading order
- Most sections are open compositions separated by rules, not cards
- The single form container uses a 14px radius; controls use a 7px radius

## Accessibility

- Body copy uses high contrast on light or dark surfaces.
- Cyan focus rings remain visible against both neutral backgrounds.
- Violet is never used for small body copy on the light background.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Semantic headings, form labels, landmarks, and descriptive links are required.

## Voice

Write in Irene's first-person voice. Prefer concrete nouns and verbs: workflow, bottleneck, decision, test, plan, build. Avoid platform, revolutionary, cutting-edge, unlock, supercharge, and vague promises about growth.
